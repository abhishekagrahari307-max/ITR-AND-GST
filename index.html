Clear scope

Useful preparation tools, with honest limits

TaxMitra helps organize information; it is not a substitute for official portal records or professional advice.

What you can do

Enter your figures, calculate estimates, prepare invoices, reconcile records, save supported drafts in this browser and export working papers.

What this site cannot do

It cannot fetch AIS, Form 26AS or GST portal data, generate a government IRN, file or submit returns, or e-verify a return.

Before relying on a result

Confirm the financial year and applicable rules, reconcile against official documents, and seek qualified advice for complex matters. AI responses may be incomplete or wrong.
