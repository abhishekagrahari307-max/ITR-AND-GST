📌 PAN Format: 10 alphanumeric characters — AAAAA9999A
• Characters 1-3: Series (any 3 alphabets)
• Character 4: Type — P=Person, C=Company, H=HUF, F=Firm, A=AOP, T=Trust, B=BOI, L=Local, J=AJP, G=Govt
• Character 5: First letter of surname
• Characters 6-9: Sequential number (0001-9999)
• Character 10: Check digit (alphabet)

Aadhaar-PAN Linking
⚠️ Important: Aadhaar-PAN linking deadline already passed (June 30, 2023). If not linked, PAN is inoperative. Fine: ₹1,000. Link now to avoid higher TDS (20%) and other issues.

Steps to Link Online

  • 1Go to IT Portal
  • 2Click Quick Links → Link Aadhaar
  • 3Enter PAN + Aadhaar + Name
  • 4Pay ₹1,000 via challan if deadline missed
  • 5Submit — OTP verification
  • 6Linking completes within 10 days

PAN Correction / Update

What Can Be Corrected?

  • ✓Name correction (spelling mistake)
  • ✓Date of Birth correction
  • ✓Father's name correction
  • ✓Address update
  • ✓Photo update
  • ✓Signature update

How to Correct

  • 1Go to NSDL/UTIITSL → "Changes/Correction in PAN"
  • 2Fill correction form (49A type)
  • 3Upload supporting documents
  • 4Pay ₹107 (Indian) or ₹1,017 (foreign)
  • 5Submit — new PAN card dispatched in 15-20 days
