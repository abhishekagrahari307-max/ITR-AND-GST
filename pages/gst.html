📖 GSTIN Structure

09AAAPL1234C1ZV
🔴 09 – State Code (UP)
🟣 AAAPL – First 5 of PAN (letters)
🟢 1234 – Next 4 of PAN (digits)
🟡 C – Last char of PAN (letter)
🔵 1 – Entity number (1-9)
🩷 Z – Always Z
🔷 V – Check digit (0-9 or A-Z)
