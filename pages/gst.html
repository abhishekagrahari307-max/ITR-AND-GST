🚛 E-Way Bill Guide

When is E-Way Bill Required?

Movement of goods > ₹50,000 value (inter-state or intra-state where notified)

Validity Period

Up to 200 km: 1 day | 200-300 km: 2 days | Every 100 km additional: +1 day

Penalty

₹10,000 or tax amount (whichever higher) + goods may be seized

Who Can Generate?

Supplier, recipient, or transporter – any of them on ewaybillgst.gov.in
