📁 TDS Returns – Form Guide

Form 24Q

TDS on salary (Section 192). Quarterly filing by employers.

Due: Q1: 31 Jul | Q2: 31 Oct | Q3: 31 Jan | Q4: 31 May

Form 26Q

TDS on non-salary payments – contractor, interest, rent, commission etc.

Due: Same as 24Q quarterly schedule

Form 27Q

TDS on payments to NRI/foreign companies. Requires DTAA knowledge.

Due: Same quarterly schedule

Form 27EQ

TCS (Tax Collected at Source) return. For sellers collecting TCS.

Due: Same quarterly schedule
