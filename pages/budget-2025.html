❓ Frequently Asked Questions

New Regime better hai ya Old Regime?

Zyada tar cases mein New Regime better hai AY 2026-27 mein, especially agar aapki gross salary ≤ ₹15.75L hai aur aapke deductions ₹2-3L se kam hain.

Old Regime better hai agar: 80C ₹1.5L + 80D ₹50K + HRA ₹80K+ + Home Loan interest ₹2L = Total deductions ₹4L+ hain.

TaxMitra Regime Calculator → se apna exact calculation karein.

12.75L tak zero tax sirf salaried ke liye hai?

Haan, ₹75,000 Standard Deduction sirf salaried employees aur pensioners ke liye hai. Business income ya freelancer ke liye ₹12L tak zero tax hoga (without standard deduction).

Business walo ke liye: ₹12L income par ₹60,000 tax banta hai, jo 87A rebate se zero ho jata hai.

87A Rebate claim karne ke liye kya karna hai?

87A Rebate automatically calculate hoti hai ITR mein. Koi alag form ya application nahi chahiye. Bas ensure karein ki aapki total taxable income ₹12 lakh se zyada nahi hai (New Regime mein).

Important: Capital Gains wali income 87A rebate se cover nahi hoti special rate wali gains ke liye.

New Regime mein HRA milta hai?

Nahi. New Regime mein HRA exemption, LTA, 80C, 80D, home loan interest deduction — ye sab allowed nahi hai.

New Regime mein sirf ye milta hai: Standard Deduction ₹75,000 (salaried ke liye) aur Professional Tax deduction.

Agar income 12L se thoda zyada hai, to kya karna chahiye?

Agar income ₹12.01L hai, to ₹60,000 rebate nahi milegi (New Regime mein). Marginal relief provision hai — aap itna hi tax denge jitna ₹12L se zyada income hai.

Example: Income ₹12.5L. Tax: ₹75,000. Rebate: ₹0. Par marginal relief: Max tax = ₹12.5L - ₹12L = ₹50,000. So actual tax = ₹50,000.

Is case mein NPS mein invest kar ke income ₹12L se neeche la sakte hain (Section 80CCD(2) employer contribution — New Regime mein allowed!).

Capital gains AY 2026-27 mein kaise taxed hogi?

Equity / Mutual Funds (STT paid):

  • STCG (holding < 12 months): 20% (Budget 2025 se — pehle 15% tha)
  • LTCG (holding > 12 months): 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25L exemption (pehle 10% above ₹1L tha)

Property: LTCG (holding > 24 months): 12.5% without indexation OR 20% with indexation (choose whichever lower)
