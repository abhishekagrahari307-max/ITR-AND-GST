Budget 2025-26 Key Highlights

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman – February 2025

✅ New Regime Standard Deduction

₹50,000 → ₹75,000 increased. Salaried individuals ko zyada benefit.

✅ Capital Gain Tax Changes

STCG equity: 15%→20% | LTCG equity: 10%→12.5% | LTCG exemption: ₹1L→₹1.25L

✅ 87A Rebate Extended

New regime mein ₹7L tak zero tax (rebate ₹25K→₹60K) effective taxable income ≤₹12L

✅ New Basic Exemption Limit

New regime: ₹4L basic nil slab (Budget 2025). Effective zero tax up to ₹12.75L (₹12L + ₹75K std ded + ₹60K rebate 87A)

✅ TDS Threshold Increased

194A interest threshold: ₹40K→₹50K. 194I rent: ₹2.4L→₹2.4L (no change)

⚠️ STT Increase

Securities Transaction Tax on F&O increased. Options: 0.0625% → 0.1%

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

ITR filing ki last date kab hai? +
Non-audit cases: 31 July | Audit cases: 31 October | Belated return (with penalty): 31 December | Revised return: 31 December. FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) deadlines same pattern follow karengi.
Old Regime vs New Regime – konsa choose karoon? +
Break-even point approximately ₹3.75 lakh in total deductions hai. Agar aapke deductions (80C + 80D + HRA + Home Loan etc.) ₹3.75L se zyada hain toh Old Regime better hai. Warna New Regime zyada tax bachata hai. TaxMitra ka calculator use karo exact comparison ke liye!
Income Tax Notice mila hai – kya karoon? +
1. Notice ki deadline note karo (usually 30 days). 2. Notice section number check karo (143(1), 143(2), 148, etc.). 3. Income Tax e-filing portal par e-Proceedings section mein respond karo. 4. Gestation period mein CA se consult karo. TaxMitra AI Notice Explainer use karo!
GST registration kab compulsory hai? +
Normal businesses: Aggregate turnover > ₹40 lakh (goods) ya ₹20 lakh (services). Special category states: ₹20L/₹10L. Inter-state supply: Registration compulsory regardless of turnover. E-commerce operators: Registration mandatory from day 1.
PAN-Aadhaar linking nahi ki – kya hoga? +
Deadline already past ho gayi hai (30 June 2023) — Link karne ke liye ₹1,000 penalty ke saath portal available hai. Ab ₹1,000 penalty deke link karo. Link nahi karne par PAN inoperative ho jaata hai, TDS double rate par katega, refund nahi milega, high-value transactions restricted honge.
