🔍 Annual Information Statement (AIS)

AIS woh information hai jo Income Tax Department ko aapke financial transactions ke baare mein milti hai — isse match karna zaroori hai. Is site se AIS download nahi ho sakta: wo aapke portal login se hi milta hai. AIS download karke totals yahan bhar dijiye — comparison aur mismatch check sab real hoga.

📥 AIS / TIS download: incometax.gov.in → login → e-File → Income Tax Return → Annual Information Statement (AIS). PDF ke totals section se neeche diye figures copy kijiye.

🔎 AIS vs your ITR draft

📄 Form 26AS – Tax Credit Statement

Form 26AS mein aapke TDS, TCS, advance tax aur self-assessment tax ki entries hoti hain. Ye site 26AS ko fetch nahi kar sakti — wo TRACES/portal login maangta hai aur browser se direct access possible nahi hai. 26AS se totals yahan bhar dijiye; total credit ka calculation real hoga.

📥 26AS view/download: incometax.gov.in → login → e-File → Income Tax Return → View Form 26AS. Salary TDS ke liye Form 16 Part A bhi dekh sakte hain.

🧾 Your tax credit summary

📋 Form 16 – salary TDS certificate

Form 16 aapka salary TDS certificate hai. PDF auto-reading (OCR) is site par nahi hai — wo server-side processing maangta hai. Part A/Part B se figures yahan bhar dijiye: ye save hote hain aur ek click mein ITR wizard ke salary schedule mein chale jaate hain.
