Not a filing portal. This wizard prepares the computation and an editable working paper only. It does not submit a return, does not prefill from the Income Tax portal, and does not replace Form 16, AIS or 26AS. Always reconcile your figures with those documents (or your CA) before filing on incometax.gov.in.
