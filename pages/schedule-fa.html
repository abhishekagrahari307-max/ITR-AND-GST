🌍 Schedule FA – foreign assets

Bank accounts, brokerage holdings, RSUs and ESPPs. Enter the foreign-currency amounts and the rule 115 rate — the rupee figures, peak values and the disclosure checklist are computed for you.

Schedule FA is a disclosure schedule in ITR-2 / ITR-3. This page prepares the working paper and the rupee conversion; it does not file anything. Non-disclosure of a foreign asset can attract a penalty under the Black Money Act, so verify every entry against your broker and bank statements before you file.
