📂 Which ITR Form?

💡 F&O Tax Planning Tips

  • ✅ Claim ALL expenses: brokerage, STT, exchange charges, internet, advisory fees
  • ✅ F&O loss can be set off against NORMAL business income (not salary)
  • ✅ Carry forward F&O loss for 8 years — MUST file ITR-3 before due date
  • ✅ Maintain proper books of accounts — trade logs from broker
  • ⚠️ F&O loss CANNOT be set off against salary income
  • ⚠️ If turnover >₹10Cr — mandatory CA audit before filing

📚 F&O Tax — Complete Guide

📊 Turnover Calculation

Turnover = Sum of all |Profit| + Sum of all |Loss| on absolute basis. NOT net profit/loss. Example: +₹5L profit + ₹3L loss = ₹8L turnover.

💡 Expenses Allowed

Brokerage, STT, Exchange charges, SEBI charges, GST on brokerage, internet charges, advisory fees, depreciation on computer/software.

⚠️ Loss Set-off Rules

F&O = Non-speculative business. Loss can set off against other business income. CANNOT set off against salary/capital gains. Carry forward 8 years.

🔴 Audit Requirements

If turnover >₹10Crore: Mandatory audit u/s 44AB. If turnover ≤₹10Cr and profit >6%: No audit. If profit <6%: Audit required (u/s 44AD not applicable).
