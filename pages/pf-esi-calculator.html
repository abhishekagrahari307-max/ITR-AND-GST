EPF Calculation
EPF Rules:
• Employee: 12% of Basic+DA → EPF Account
• Employer: 12% → split: 8.33% EPS + 3.67% EPF
• EPS ceiling: ₹1,250/month (on ₹15,000 base)
• PF Interest FY 2025-26: 8.25% per annum
• Withdrawal: Tax-free after 5 continuous years

ESI Calculation
ESI Applicability:
• Gross salary ≤ ₹21,000/month → ESI mandatory
• Employee contribution: 0.75% of gross
• Employer contribution: 3.25% of gross
• Total: 4% of gross salary
• Benefits: Medical, disability, maternity, death

Gratuity Calculation
Gratuity Formula:
= (Basic+DA × 15 × Years) / 26
• Payable after 5 years of service
• Max exempt: ₹20 lakhs
• For non-covered: use 30 instead of 26
• Months > 6 rounded up to 1 year

Statutory Bonus
Payment of Bonus Act:
• Applicable if Basic+DA ≤ ₹21,000/month
• Minimum bonus: 8.33% of salary
• Max bonus: 20% of salary
• Calculation ceiling: ₹7,000 or min wage (higher)
• Payable within 8 months of financial year end
