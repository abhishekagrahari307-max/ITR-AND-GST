📚 Income Tax Notice Types — Complete Guide

143(1) — Intimation
🟢 LOW RISK. Routine processing. Demand or refund note. Check Form 26AS. Respond within 30 days online if disagree. Usually automated.
143(2) — Scrutiny
🟡 MEDIUM RISK. Detailed examination. Submit all documents online within deadline. Can oppose if no valid reason. CA recommended.
148 — Reassessment
🔴 HIGH RISK. Income escaped assessment. Immediately engage CA. Time limit: 3-10 years. Respond within 30 days mandatory.
156 — Demand
🟡 MEDIUM RISK. Outstanding tax demand. Pay within 30 days or file rectification u/s 154. Late payment: 1%/month interest u/s 220(2).
245 — Refund Adjusted
🟡 LOW-MEDIUM. Refund adjusted against old demand. Verify old demand, file response if demand was already paid.
139(9) — Defective Return
🟡 LOW RISK. ITR has technical defects. Resubmit corrected return within 15 days. Otherwise ITR treated as void.
