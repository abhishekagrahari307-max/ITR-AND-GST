Section 154 — Rectification of Mistake Sec 154

When to File?

Arithmetic Mistake in Order
Calculation error by AO in assessment order
✓ Eligible
Wrong Tax Rate Applied
Wrong slab, wrong surcharge, wrong cess
✓ Eligible
Deduction Not Given
80C/80D claimed in ITR but not allowed
✓ Common case
Time Limit: 4 Years
From end of FY in which order was passed
⏱ 4 years

Steps to File

1. Login to incometax.gov.in
2. e-File → Rectification → Income Tax Return
3. Select AY → Choose Rectification Type
4. Types: Reprocess / Schedules / Donation/Capital Gains
5. Submit with explanation → AO must respond in 6 months
⚠️ Remember: Sec 154 is ONLY for "mistake apparent from record" — not for debatable points or new claims. For those, file appeal u/s 246A.
CIT(A) Appeal — Section 246A Form 35

Appealable Orders

  • ✅ Assessment order u/s 143(3)
  • ✅ Reassessment order u/s 147
  • ✅ Penalty order u/s 271/272
  • ✅ Intimation u/s 143(1) — limited grounds
  • ✅ Order refusing registration u/s 12AA
  • ❌ Preliminary inquiry notices NOT appealable

Filing Details

Form: Form 35 (online only)
Time Limit: 30 days from receipt of order
Fees: Assessed income < ₹1L → ₹250 | ₹1L-₹2L → ₹500 | >₹2L → ₹1,000
Portal: e-File → Income Tax Forms → Form 35
20% of demand: May need to deposit 20% of disputed tax

Document Checklist for CIT(A)

Copy of Assessment Order
Copy of ITR filed
Form 16/16A
Notice under which assessment made
Grounds of appeal (written)
Evidence supporting your claim
Bank statements
Tax computation working
💡 Faceless Appeals: All CIT(A) proceedings are now faceless under Faceless Appeal Scheme 2021 — no personal hearing by default. Upload all documents online.
ITAT — Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (Sec 253) Form 36

Who Can File?

  • ✅ Assessee (taxpayer) against CIT(A) order
  • ✅ Department against CIT(A) order
  • ⏱ Time limit: 60 days from CIT(A) order
  • 📋 Form 36 (assessee) / Form 36A (Dept)

Filing Fees

• Assessed income ≤ ₹1L: ₹500
• ₹1L to ₹2L: ₹1,500
• ₹2L to ₹5L: ₹5,000
• Above ₹5L: ₹10,000
• Penalty/interest only: ₹500
📌 Important: ITAT is the last fact-finding body. High Court / Supreme Court only on questions of law. Consider hiring a CA/Advocate for ITAT proceedings.
Penalty Waiver — Section 273A/273B

Sec 273A — Waiver of Penalty

  • ✅ Penalty under 270A, 271 etc.
  • ✅ Voluntary disclosure before detection
  • ✅ Cooperation with AO proceedings
  • ✅ No previous penalty conviction
  • 📋 Apply to PCIT / CIT

Sec 273B — Reasonable Cause

  • ✅ Delay due to illness/death in family
  • ✅ Natural disaster / COVID impact
  • ✅ Bank strike / technical issues
  • ✅ Incorrect professional advice
  • 📋 Attach supporting documents

Stay of Demand — Section 220(6)

जब assessment demand आए और appeal file करें, तब demand stay ke liye application:

Step 1: Appeal file करें CIT(A) में u/s 246A
Step 2: AO को Sec 220(6) under stay application दें — 20% deposit की request
Step 3: Generally, 20% pay करने पर demand stay हो जाती है during CIT(A) pendency
Step 4: ITAT में भी stay application — typically 15-20% deposit
✅ CBDT Circular 1914: No coercive recovery during stay period if cooperation given
Faceless Appeal Scheme 2021

Key Features

  • All appeals now online — no physical appearance
  • Random allocation to CIT(A) officers
  • No territorial jurisdiction
  • All communication via email/portal
  • Video conferencing for personal hearings

How to Submit Documents

  • Login IT Portal → e-Proceedings → Appeal
  • Submit via Response to Outstanding Demand
  • Upload as PDF/JPEG — max 50MB per file
  • Submit within response time given
  • Track status in portal dashboard
🔗 IT Portal: incometax.gov.in → Login → e-File → Income Tax Forms → Form 35
