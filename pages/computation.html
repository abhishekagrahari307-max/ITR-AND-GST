ITR Filing + Computation Guide 2026-27

Developed by Abhishek Agrahari | Budget 2025 Updated | AY 2026-27

📌

ITR Filing Kya Hai?

Income Tax Return (ITR) ek form hai jo aap Income Tax Department ko submit karte ho — apni income, deductions aur tax details ke saath. AY 2026-27 ka matlab hai FY 2025-26 (1 April 2025 – 31 March 2026) ki income.

✅

Filing Zaroori Kab?

  • Annual income > ₹2.5 lakh (old) / ₹3 lakh (senior)
  • TDS refund chahiye
  • Carry forward losses (capital gains etc.)
  • Visa/loan ke liye income proof
  • Foreign assets/income hai
  • Director in company ya foreign asset holder
🎁

Budget 2025 Highlights

  • Zero Tax up to ₹12,75,000 salary (New Regime)
  • 87A Rebate ₹60,000 (if income ≤ ₹12L)
  • Standard Deduction ₹75,000 (New Regime)
  • New Regime is now DEFAULT
  • No 80C/HRA in New Regime but slab rate lower
⚡

Zero Tax Formula

Gross Salary₹12,75,000
(–) Standard Deduction₹75,000
Net Taxable Income₹12,00,000
Tax on ₹12L (slabs)₹60,000
(–) 87A Rebate₹60,000
NET TAX PAYABLE₹0 🎉

ITR-1 (Sahaj)

Most Common

Who: Resident Individual only

  • ✅ Salary / Pension income
  • ✅ 1 House Property
  • ✅ Other Sources (FD interest, savings)
  • ✅ Agricultural income ≤ ₹5,000
  • ⚠️ Income limit: ≤ ₹50 Lakh
  • ❌ NOT for: Capital gains, foreign assets
  • ❌ NOT for: Director in company
  • ❌ NOT for: NRI
📄 Documents: Form 16, Form 26AS, AIS, Bank statement
📊

ITR-2

Capital Gains

Who: Individual / HUF

  • ✅ All ITR-1 income
  • ✅ Capital Gains (shares, property, MF)
  • ✅ 2+ House Properties
  • ✅ Foreign income / assets
  • ✅ Director in company (no business income)
  • ✅ Unlisted shares
  • ❌ NOT for: Business/Profession income
📄 Capital Gain statement from broker, Form 16, Form 26AS
🏭

ITR-3

Business (Full Books)

Who: Individual / HUF with business

  • ✅ Business / Profession income (full books)
  • ✅ All ITR-2 incomes
  • ✅ Partner in firm
  • ✅ P&L and Balance Sheet required
  • ⚠️ Audit: if turnover > ₹1 Cr (business) / ₹50L (profession)
📄 P&L account, Balance Sheet, Audit report (if applicable)
🏪

ITR-4 (Sugam)

Presumptive

Who: Individual / HUF / Firm (not LLP)

  • ✅ 44AD: Business turnover ≤ ₹3 Cr → 8%/6% income
  • ✅ 44ADA: Professional receipts ≤ ₹75L → 50% income
  • ✅ 44AE: Goods carriage owner ≤ 10 vehicles
  • ✅ Salary + presumptive income both allowed
  • ❌ NOT if: opted out of presumptive scheme
📄 Bank statement, GST returns, basic records
🤝

ITR-5

Who: Firms, LLPs, AOPs, BOIs, Cooperative Societies, Investment Funds, Estate of deceased

  • ✅ Partnership firms, LLPs
  • ✅ Association of Persons (AOP)
  • ✅ Body of Individuals (BOI)
  • ⚠️ NOT for: Individuals, HUF, Companies
🏢

ITR-6 & ITR-7

ITR-6: Companies (other than claiming Sec 11/12 exemption). Requires DSC.

ITR-7: Trusts, Political Parties, Charitable/Religious institutions, Scientific Research associations. Section 11/12 exemption claimants.

📊 When to Choose Which Regime?

✅ New Regime Better When:
  • Investments/deductions < ₹3.75 lakh total
  • Salary ≤ ₹12.75L (ZERO TAX!)
  • Less 80C investments done
  • Rented accommodation (no HRA claim needed)
  • No home loan
  • Young earner with few deductions
✅ Old Regime Better When:
  • Deductions > ₹3.75 lakh total
  • High HRA (metro city, high rent)
  • Home loan interest ≥ ₹2 lakh
  • Max 80C + NPS + parents health insurance
  • Income > ₹15L with maximum deductions
💡 Break-Even Point: If total deductions (80C + 80D + HRA + Home loan + NPS + Standard Deduction difference) > ₹3,75,000 → Old Regime saves more. Use TaxMitra's Regime Comparison above to get exact numbers!

🧮 Step-by-Step Tax Computation Process

Yeh wahi process hai jo Chartered Accountants follow karte hain — TaxMitra automatically yeh sab calculate karta hai!

1

Gross Total Income Calculate Karo

5 heads of income jodo: Salary + House Property + Business/Profession + Capital Gains + Other Sources

Gross Total Income = Σ All 5 Heads of Income
2

Deductions Ghataao (Chapter VI-A)

New Regime: Only Standard Deduction (₹75K) + 80CCD(2)
Old Regime: 80C, 80D, HRA, Home Loan, 80CCD(1B), 80E, 80G etc.

Total Income = GTI − Deductions
3

Tax on Total Income (Slab-wise)

Apply applicable slab rates on total taxable income. For New Regime: Budget 2025 slabs. For Old Regime: old slabs.

Tax = Σ (Slab Amount × Slab Rate)
4

Surcharge Add Karo (if income > ₹50L)

Income ₹50L–₹1Cr: 10% surcharge | ₹1Cr–₹2Cr: 15% | ₹2Cr–₹5Cr: 25% | Above ₹5Cr: 37% (max ₹25L for short-term CG)

Tax After Surcharge = Tax + (Tax × Surcharge %)
5

Health & Education Cess (4%)

Sabse end mein 4% cess lagta hai — tax + surcharge dono pe. Cess pe koi deduction nahi milta.

Total Tax = (Tax + Surcharge) × 1.04
6

87A Rebate Ghataao

New Regime: Income ≤ ₹12L → ₹60,000 rebate (full tax waived)
Old Regime: Income ≤ ₹5L → ₹12,500 rebate
Note: Capital Gains (STCG/LTCG) pe 87A rebate nahi milta

Tax After Rebate = Max(0, Total Tax − 87A Rebate)
7

TDS / Advance Tax Ghataao → Net Payable/Refund

Form 26AS aur Form 16 se TDS figure lo. Advance tax already paid tha to wo bhi ghataao.

Net Payable = Total Tax − TDS − Advance Tax − TCS + Interest 234A/B/C
✓

Self Assessment Tax Pay Karo (if net payable > 0)

incometax.gov.in → e-Pay Tax (Challan 280) → Self Assessment Tax. BSR code aur challan serial number note karo ITR mein daalne ke liye.

Section 80C — Max ₹1,50,000

  • ELSS MF — 3 yr lock-in, market-linked returns (12-18%)
  • PPF — 15 yr, 7.1% guaranteed, tax-free maturity
  • EPF — Auto deducted, employer matches
  • LIC Premium — Life insurance (own/spouse/children)
  • NSC — 5 yr, 6.8% post office scheme
  • 5yr Tax Saver FD — Bank/PO FD (5 yr lock-in)
  • Sukanya Samriddhi — 8.2%, for girl child
  • Tuition fees — 2 children school/college fees
  • Home Loan Principal — Repayment of principal
  • Stamp Duty — On property purchase
⚠️ New Regime mein 80C nahi milta
🏥

Section 80D — Health Insurance

  • Self + Family (below 60): ₹25,000
  • Self + Family (60+): ₹50,000
  • Parents (below 60): ₹25,000
  • Parents (60+): ₹50,000
  • Max total: ₹25K+₹50K = ₹75,000
  • Preventive health checkup: ₹5,000 (within above limit)
  • Cash payment allowed only for preventive checkup
🏦

NPS — National Pension System

  • 80CCD(1): 10% of salary (within 80C ₹1.5L cap)
  • 80CCD(1B): Extra ₹50,000 over 80C — Old Regime only!
  • 80CCD(2): Employer NPS — BOTH regimes! 10% of salary (14% govt)
  • Tier-1 account: lock-in till 60
  • 60% maturity: tax-free | 40%: annuity (taxable)
✅ 80CCD(2) New Regime mein bhi milta hai — best deduction!
🏠

HRA + Home Loan

HRA Exemption = Min of:

  • Actual HRA received
  • Rent paid − 10% of Basic Salary
  • 50% of Basic (Metro) / 40% of Basic (Non-Metro)

Home Loan u/s 24(b):

  • Interest on SOP: Max ₹2,00,000
  • Interest on LOP: No limit (set off limit ₹2L)
  • Pre-construction interest: 1/5 per year for 5 years
📚

Other Key Deductions

  • 80E: Education loan interest — Full amount, no cap, 8 years
  • 80EEA: Housing loan interest (affordable) — ₹1.5L extra
  • 80G: Donations — 50% or 100% of donation (org dependent)
  • 80GG: Rent paid (no HRA) — Min(₹5K/month, 25% income, rent−10%income), max ₹60K
  • 80TTA: Savings interest — Max ₹10,000 (below 60)
  • 80TTB: All interest — Max ₹50,000 (60+ only)
  • 80U / 80DD: Disability — ₹75K–₹1.25L
  • 80DDB: Medical treatment (specified) — ₹40K–₹1L
💡

Tax Saving Strategy 2026

  • Salary ≤ ₹12.75L → New Regime → ZERO TAX (nothing to plan!)
  • Salary ₹12.75L–₹15L → Use TaxMitra's Regime Comparison
  • Salary > ₹15L with high deductions → Old Regime check karo
  • Max 80CCD(2) employer NPS in both regimes
  • Old Regime: 80C ₹1.5L + NPS ₹50K + 80D ₹75K + HRA + Home Loan = ₹5L+ savings

Capital Gains Exemptions

  • u/s 54: Sell residential property → Buy new residential property (within 2 yr / construct 3 yr)
  • u/s 54F: Sell any asset → Buy residential property (full proceeds reinvest for full exemption)
  • u/s 54EC: Invest ₹50L in NHAI/REC bonds (within 6 months, lock-in 5 yr)
  • u/s 54B: Agricultural land → Buy agricultural land
  • LTCG Equity ₹1.25L: First ₹1.25L per year tax-free (auto exemption)
📊

Indexation (Cost Inflation Index)

Indexation se purchase cost badh jaati hai → capital gain kam hota hai → less tax!

Formula: Indexed Cost = Purchase Price × (CII of Sale Year ÷ CII of Purchase Year)
FY 2024-25 CII: 363 | FY 2023-24: 348 | FY 2022-23: 331
⚠️ Budget 2024: Property LTCG can choose 12.5% WITHOUT indexation OR 20% WITH indexation (whichever lower)
📄

Form 16 — Salary TDS Certificate

  • Part A: Employer details, TAN, Employee PAN, TDS deposited quarter-wise
  • Part B: Salary computation — Gross Salary, deductions, taxable salary, tax calculation
  • Issued by employer by: 15 June every year
  • Not received? Contact HR — employer is legally required to issue
  • Multiple employers → multiple Form 16 → add all salaries
Download: From employer's HR/payroll portal
🔍

Form 26AS — Tax Credit Statement

  • Part A: TDS by employers, banks, others
  • Part B: TDS on non-salary
  • Part C: Self-assessment / advance tax paid
  • Part D: Tax demand / refund
  • Part G: TCS collected
  • Always verify Form 16 vs Form 26AS — mismatches = notices!
Download: incometax.gov.in → e-File → View 26AS
📊

AIS — Annual Information Statement

  • More comprehensive than 26AS
  • Shows: Salary, Interest, Dividends, MF transactions, Property
  • Gives "Reported" and "Modified" values
  • IT dept uses AIS to send notices for unreported income
  • If AIS shows wrong info → submit feedback on portal
Download: incometax.gov.in → Services → Annual Information Statement

Late Filing Consequences

  • Late fee u/s 234F: Income ≤ ₹5L → ₹1,000 | Income > ₹5L → ₹5,000
  • Interest u/s 234A: 1% per month on tax payable from due date
  • Losses lapse: Capital loss / business loss can't be carried forward if filed late
  • No revision: Belated return can't be revised
  • Visa issues: Belated ITR not accepted by many embassies

LOW RISK

u/s 143(1) — Intimation

Routine processing notice. ITR processed ho gaya, koi adjustment hua ya nahi.

  • Demand 0 → No action needed
  • Demand > 0 → Form 26AS se TDS verify karo
  • Agree to demand → Pay within 30 days
  • Disagree → Online response submit karo u/s 154
Response: incometax.gov.in → e-Proceedings
MEDIUM RISK

u/s 143(2) — Scrutiny

Detailed examination selected. Income Tax Officer will review your return.

  • Respond within 30 days (time limit strict)
  • Gather all income proofs, investment proofs
  • CA se consult recommended
  • Don't ignore — penalty + ex-parte assessment
Response: Online via e-Proceedings within deadline
HIGH RISK

u/s 148 — Reassessment

IT dept believes income escaped assessment. Serious notice — act immediately.

  • CA se immediately milein
  • 30 days mein respond karna mandatory
  • Original ITR + all proofs ready rakho
  • If income truly escaped → voluntary disclosure better
Immediately engage a CA — don't respond alone
DEMAND

u/s 156 — Demand Notice

Outstanding tax demand. Pay within 30 days or challenge if wrong.

  • Verify demand via Form 26AS
  • Agree → Pay via Challan 280
  • Disagree → File rectification u/s 154
  • Late payment: 1%/month interest u/s 220(2)
