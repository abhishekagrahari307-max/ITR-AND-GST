Contact Me 📍 Lucknow, UP

Purpose and limits

The goal is to make common tax and GST preparation tasks easier to review in a browser.

Tools and integrations vary by page. Some save data only in your browser, and AI features may require an external provider key. No page on this site files or submits a return to a government portal.

Available tool categories

ITR preparation

Calculations and working papers; no return submission.

GST and bookkeeping

Local drafts and reconciliation tools; no GST portal filing.

Calculators and guides

Reference estimates and educational material. Verify current rules.

Optional AI features

Availability depends on the page and provider configuration. Review provider privacy and fees.

Data and integrations

Data handling depends on the tool. Some features save information in browser storage; optional AI providers may receive prompts or documents you choose to send. Review the privacy notice and each feature's on-screen explanation before entering sensitive information.

Do not treat a browser draft, AI response or generated file as an official filing receipt or portal-validated return.

⚠️ Important Note

TaxMitra is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Income Tax Department, GST Council or any government portal. Tools may contain errors or use assumptions that do not fit your circumstances. Verify all information against current official sources and seek qualified professional advice when needed. Do not rely on this site as a filing receipt, legal opinion or guarantee of an outcome.
